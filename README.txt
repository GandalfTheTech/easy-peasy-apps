EASY-PEASY APPS — ENGLISH WEBSITE BASE
======================================

Files:
- index.html — English homepage, focused on your own digital products
- privacy-policy.html — public privacy-policy template
- terms-of-use.html — basic terms template
- styles.css — responsive styling
- script.js — mobile menu and current year

Before publishing:
1. Replace CONTACT@YOURDOMAIN.COM and every [placeholder].
2. Decide which exact apps/services this policy covers.
3. Confirm the legal identity and contact details of the data controller.
4. Inventory all data collected by each app and every third-party SDK/provider.
5. Add real retention periods, legal bases, providers, transfer details and cookie information.
6. Check the live website for cookies, analytics, external fonts, embedded content and forms.
7. Verify current Google Play and Apple App Store requirements for each app, including privacy disclosures and account/data deletion where applicable.
8. Upload all files to your web host. Keep them in the same directory.

The website is positioned around your own products, bug reports, ideas and improvements. It does not advertise a general app-development agency or promise to accept commissioned work.

The privacy policy is a starter template, not legal advice or a guarantee of GDPR compliance. It must accurately reflect actual practices and should be reviewed for your circumstances. The current CSS loads Google Fonts externally; self-host the fonts if you prefer to avoid that third-party request.
